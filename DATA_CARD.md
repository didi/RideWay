# RideWay Data Card

## Overview

RideWay contains Chinese-language ride-hailing benchmark tasks, simulated database states, model-generated interaction trajectories, and human preference labels used to study successful and efficient tool use. All released interactions take place in a stateful simulation; the repository does not provide access to a live ride-hailing service or production system.

## Provenance and construction

The task scenarios are constructed from abstracted ride-hailing interaction patterns and are designed to cover clarification, location grounding, option comparison, preference preservation, booking, and booking revision. Released trajectories are generated by LLM agents and a fixed user simulator operating against synthetic benchmark states.

No raw production conversation, trip, account, payment, device-location, or dispatch log is intentionally included in the release. Human annotations express preferences between benchmark trajectories and are released without annotator identity information.

## Synthetic identifiers and locations

The data contains realistic-looking fields needed to exercise ride-service tools, including:

- passenger and driver names;
- phone-number-like strings;
- vehicle plates and vehicle descriptions;
- driver, order, and flight identifiers;
- home, work, pickup, drop-off, and waypoint addresses;
- trip histories, preferences, and scheduling details.

These fields are synthetic or fictional test fixtures and are not intended to correspond to real people, accounts, vehicles, or trips. Some city names, transport hubs, hospitals, businesses, residential developments, streets, and other public POIs may be real. Their presence provides geographic context only and does not imply that a named person lives at, works at, visited, or booked a trip involving that location. Any accidental resemblance between a synthetic record and a real person or transaction is coincidental.

## Intended use

The data is intended for research and evaluation of tool-using language-model agents, including task completion, interaction efficiency, multi-turn state tracking, and rubric-based evaluation. It may also be used to reproduce the aggregate benchmark analyses included in this repository, subject to the repository license.

The data is not intended for identifying individuals, inferring sensitive attributes, operating a real dispatch service, making safety-critical decisions, or providing factual travel, medical, financial, or legal guidance.

## Limitations and risks

Trajectories contain model-generated text and may include hallucinations, stereotypes, unsafe suggestions, outdated facts, or incidental references to third-party brands and services. Real-world place names may change, and simulated prices, routes, availability, driver attributes, and policies must not be interpreted as current service information.

Users who combine RideWay with other datasets are responsible for reassessing privacy and re-identification risks. Public redistribution should preserve this data card and must not present synthetic records as authentic user or driver activity.

## Reporting concerns

If any released record appears to contain genuine personal, confidential, proprietary, or security-sensitive information, stop redistributing that record and report it through the repository's GitHub issue tracker. Include the file path and record identifier, but do not repost the potentially sensitive value in a public issue. Maintainers will review the report and remove or replace affected material when appropriate.
